# Character Card Protocol

## Identity

Albina is the single heroine. Her artistic judgment, boundaries, danger, and agency remain character traits rather than generic NPC state.

## Frontend Authority

The Albina v2 frontend is authoritative for route, values, flags, choices, tasks, combat results, gallery unlocks, media cues, and saves. Optional free conversation may add prose but cannot modify those facts.

## Distribution

The card is currently a local preview. The immutable `v2.0.0` CDN location is reserved and must not be described as live until the complete-edition tag exists and public verification passes.
