# Albina Canon Audit

This audit covers Albina's sourced story appearances in Canto IX: the 9-18 first encounter, the 9-37 Ring battle, and the 9-43 final outcome, with 9-14 retained only as Corporism context. It does not reproduce every line or unrelated event in Canto IX. Copyrighted dialogue is not copied into the repository; canon scenes use short Chinese paraphrases and retain source locators for review.

## Evidence Policy

ProjectMoon announcements, the official channel, and the game terms are the primary publication and rights sources. The current wiki.gg transcript revisions are secondary text locators. Bilibili recordings are corroborating visual and localization locators only. Their media is not downloaded, copied, or redistributed.

Runtime content uses five classifications: `canon_exact`, `canon_paraphrase`, `supported_inference`, `AU_extension`, and `rejected`. Published scenes may not use `rejected`. Every canon paraphrase must resolve to known claims and source records during compilation. Existing romance and rescue routes are `AU_extension`, because canon does not provide a survival or timeline-split mechanism after the Ring corridor battle.

## Corrected Canon

Albina is a Corporism apprentice of the Ring, a member of the House of Spiders, and a pupil of Callisto. Her body is a white full-body prosthetic with a light-gray artificial high ponytail, a black right eye, and a white left eye. Her iron-maiden armor is white with bright yellow and gold details.

Fascia is a living greatsword made from Albina's original flesh. Albina retains mostly her brain, while Fascia is the part that lacks a brain. Albina is calm, soft-spoken, unusually honest, socially awkward, and treats prospective friends as possible ingredients for Fascia.

The card now begins with a sourced recap of the Corporism context, then Albina's 9-18 first appearance: she and Ren hold off the Sinners while Shiomi Yoru approaches and removes the Golden Boughs, and Ren later stops Albina from deviating from their plan for Fascia. The recap then covers the 9-37 Ring corridor encounter, Callisto's intervention, and Alyssa's counterattack. The fixed outcome occurs later in 9-43, not 9-37: Future Sinclair destroys Fascia and kills Albina; Callisto is killed afterward. Only after the 9-43 outcome is shown does the card expose its three author-created IF routes and nine author-created endings.

## Rejected Material

The former card content that described a silver left eye, fabricated "official dialogue", an expanded `Limbus Company Administration`, Sinclair as sinner number ten, invented Pinky doctrine, an invented childhood, a detailed House of Spiders timetable, or unsourced facility layouts is not accepted as canon. It has been removed from the active character book and the release worldbook surface.
