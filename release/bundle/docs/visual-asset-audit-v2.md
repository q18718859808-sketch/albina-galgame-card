# Visual Asset Audit V2

This pass replaces the old primary raster art batch. UI SVG plates are retained as interface chrome only; no SVG is accepted as character, CG, background, or avatar art.

## Reference Calibration

- Albina must read as an adult woman tied to the Ring and House of Spiders context.
- Required Albina identity anchors: white artificial/prosthetic body, black mechanical joints or cables at neck/chest/thighs/palms, gold highlights, white/yellow iron-maiden armor cues, chained golden spiked headband, white mask with golden eye motifs, and Fascia as a large white-gold blade with skeletal/ribbed interior hints.
- The male protagonist must read as an adult black-haired handsome man in dark formal/tactical urban clothing, not a school-age design.
- Supporting characters must be usable silhouettes for RP scenes and must not replace the Albina focus.

## Locked Style

- CG illustration.
- Hard urban action battle narrative, comparable in energy to modern high-end action gacha combat key art.
- Cold lethal atmosphere, battlefield ruins, hard cinematic light contrast, sharp combat dynamics, dark cold dominant palette, film storyboard composition.
- Romance scenes must still keep danger, restraint, rain, metal, glass, neon, gallery, lab, or battlefield tension.

## Rejection Rules

- Reject generic white-haired knights or soft fantasy princess designs.
- Reject cute moe, school-age, chibi, mascot, comedic, or pastel-dominant outputs.
- Reject missing Fascia, missing mechanical body cues, missing gold/white armor cues, or missing Ring/gallery/medical-war atmosphere for Albina sheets.
- Reject overt sexualization, nudity, minors, coercion, hypnotic control, bondage-as-sex framing, or incapacitated erotic framing.
- Reject official/wiki image files as packaged assets. References may guide shape and costume only; released assets must be generated originals.
- Reject low-resolution, text/watermark/logo contamination, obvious panel borders inside final crops, broken transparent sprites, green chroma spill, or image paths not listed in `assets/manifest.json`.

## Technical Gates

- Albina sprites: 18 transparent PNG files, `900x1200`, RGBA.
- Protagonist sprites: 12 transparent PNG files, `900x1200`, RGBA.
- Supporting sprites: at least 10 transparent PNG files; this bundle targets 12, `900x1200`, RGBA.
- CG: 24 JPG files, `1280x720`.
- Backgrounds: 12 JPG files, `1280x720`.
- Contact sheets must be generated for manual screening after processing.
- Frontend and release checks must pass after manifest replacement.
